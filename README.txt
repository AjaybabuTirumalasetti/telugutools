TELUGUTOOLS SEO BATCH

This batch adds 15 dedicated calculator pages + About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Terms/Disclaimer, and updates sitemap.xml and robots.txt.

UPLOAD:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. In GitHub repo AjaybabuTirumalasetti/telugutools choose Add file > Upload files.
3. Upload the 19 folders and replace sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
4. Commit changes.
5. Open homepage-seo-links.html and copy its section into your existing index.html after the intro and before the calculator grid.
6. Commit index.html.
7. In Google Search Console, open Sitemaps and submit the existing sitemap URL again. Do not request indexing for all 15 pages manually; the sitemap is enough.

IMPORTANT:
- Your existing homepage calculator code is not replaced by this batch.
- Keep your current Google verification meta tag in index.html.
- Before monetization, review Privacy Policy/Terms and add your preferred contact details if needed.
